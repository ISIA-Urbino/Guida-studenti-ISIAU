### Applying to ISIA Urbino as an International Student

ISIA Urbino welcomes applications from international students interested in joining our design programmes. If you wish to apply, please send the following documents to [staff.international@isiaurbino.net](mailto:staff.international@isiaurbino.net):

- **Application form**, the official form provided by ISIA Urbino, duly completed and signed ([download it here](../assets/ISIAUrbino_Application-form-for-incoming-students.pdf){:target="_blank"})
- **Portfolio**, a selection of your design, visual or creative work, in PDF format or as a link to an online portfolio
- **Motivation letter**, a short text (max one page) explaining your background, your interest in ISIA Urbino and the reasons why you wish to study with us
- **Curriculum vitae**, including your education, work experience and any relevant projects or skills

### How to submit

Please send all documents in a single email to staff.international@isiaurbino.net](mailto:staff.international@isiaurbino.net), attaching files in PDF format whenever possible. Make sure the subject line clearly states your full name and the programme you are applying for.

### Questions?

For any questions about the application process, eligibility, programmes or life at ISIA Urbino, feel free to write to [staff.international@isiaurbino.net](mailto:staff.international@isiaurbino.net). Our International Office will be happy to assist you.
